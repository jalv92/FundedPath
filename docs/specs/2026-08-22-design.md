# Funded Path — design and build contract

**Date:** 2026-08-22 · **Status:** approved, building phase 1
**What it is:** a standalone NinjaTrader 8 add-on window that tells you, while you trade, whether you
are still passing a prop-firm challenge — and what breaks first.

Phase 1 models exactly one firm: **Lucid Trading, LucidPro**, all four sizes, all three phases.
The other three firms appear in the dropdown, disabled, labelled "not modelled yet".

## 1. The rulebook (LucidPro)

Source of truth: the trader's own Lucid dashboard, captured 2026-08-22, cross-checked against
`projects/Trading/PropSim/prop_rules.json` (retrieved 2026-07-25) and two independent rule
aggregators. Where they disagree, the dashboard wins and the disagreement is recorded.

> **Amended 2026-08-22 — read [`docs/rules-sources.md`](../rules-sources.md) instead of this
> section for any rule value.** The verification pass against Lucid's help centre moved three of
> them (the 25K daily loss limit, what the LucidScale 60% is a percentage of, and the fixed limit
> below the Initial Trail Balance) and left four disagreements open. This section is kept verbatim
> as the record of what was approved; it is no longer the record of what ships.

### 1.1 Evaluation (`Phase.Evaluation`)

| Size | Start | Profit target | Max loss limit | DLL (optional) | Max size |
|------|-------|---------------|----------------|----------------|----------|
| 25K  | 25,000  | 1,250 | 1,000 | 600 or OFF   | 2 mini / 20 micro |
| 50K  | 50,000  | 3,000 | 2,000 | 1,200 or OFF | 4 mini / 40 micro |
| 100K | 100,000 | 6,000 | 3,000 | 1,800 or OFF | 6 mini / 60 micro |
| 150K | 150,000 | 9,000 | 4,500 | 2,700 or OFF | 10 mini / 100 micro |

- **Drawdown type: end-of-day trailing.** The floor is `max(EOD closing balances) − MaxLoss`.
  It ratchets **only** on a session close, never on an intraday high.
- **Floor lock:** the floor stops trailing at `start + 100` — equivalently, the trail stops once a
  day closes at `start + MaxLoss + 100`. Uniform across sizes (150K: 154,600 → floor 150,100).
- **Daily loss limit:** chosen at checkout, ON or OFF. When ON it is a **soft** breach: trading locks
  until the next session, the account survives. The trader's 50K has it **OFF**.
- **Consistency: none in the evaluation.**
- **Minimum trading days: none.** A one-day pass is allowed.
- Fee is one-time, no rebills, no time limit.

### 1.2 Live Sim / funded (`Phase.LiveSim`)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Payout profit target | $500 minimum payout |
| Max loss limit | same as the size's eval MLL, end-of-day trailing, locks at `start + 100` |
| Buffer | `start + MaxLoss + 100` (50K → 52,100). Not withdrawable. |
| DLL below the initial trail | NONE — *superseded by amendment A2: the evaluation's fixed amount for the size, if the account was bought with the limit ON* |
| LucidScale DLL above the initial trail | 60% of peak EOD balance — *superseded by amendment A3: 60% of peak EOD **profit***. Displayed only, never enforced |
| Consistency | 40% — largest day ≤ 40% of total profit. Blocks the **payout**, not the account. |
| Days to payout | 3 |
| Payouts to Live | 5 |
| Profit split | 90/10 |

**The LucidScale DLL is displayed but never enforced in phase 1.** Two sources disagree about what
the 60% is a percentage *of*, and a July capture claims a $1,200 fixed DLL below the buffer that the
current dashboard says is NONE. Both are surfaced in the UI as "needs confirming with support"
rather than silently modelled. See §7.

> *Amended 2026-08-22 (A2, A3). Both questions were answered: the 60% is of peak end-of-day
> **profit**, and the fixed limit below the Initial Trail Balance is the evaluation's amount for the
> size when the account was bought with the limit ON. The LucidScale limit is still displayed only.
> Full evidence in [`docs/rules-sources.md`](../rules-sources.md) D2 and D3.*

### 1.3 Live (`Phase.Live`)

Starts at **$0**. Max loss limit $2,000 end-of-day, floor locks at `+2,000` of profit.
Live bonus: reach $2,100 of profit → $2,000 is paid out. Marked unverified.

### 1.4 Breach basis — the one open assumption

Lucid's help centre says "balance" everywhere and never "unrealized"; a search for that word returns
zero articles. Four of the five firms researched for PropSim breach on unrealized equity.

The engine therefore takes `BreachBasis` as an explicit setting:
- `BreachBasis.Equity` (**default**) — realized + unrealized. Strict; warns earlier.
- `BreachBasis.Balance` — realized only, the literal reading of Lucid's wording.

The UI labels the resulting number as an assumption, not a fact.

## 2. Account phases and the Untracked default

`Account.Provider` distinguishes `Playback` and `Simulator` from a broker connection — and nothing
more. An Evaluation account, a Live Sim account and the trader's own personal live account all arrive
over a broker connection and are indistinguishable to NinjaTrader.

Therefore **the phase is assigned explicitly, once per NT8 account, and persisted**:

| Cockpit state | How it is decided | Colour |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|
| `Replay`   | `Account.Provider == Provider.Playback` and the account has a binding | `#F2B33D` |
| `Evaluation` | binding says Evaluation | `#4A7DFF` |
| `LiveSim`  | binding says LiveSim | `#27D67B` |
| `Live`     | binding says Live | `#9B7BFF` |
| `Untracked` | **no binding — the default for every account** | `#4E5A74` |

An unbound account is measured by nothing and recorded nowhere. The trader's own live account can
never be counted by accident.

**Ledgers never mix.** The ledger key is `Provider + "|" + AccountDisplayName`, so a Playback rehearsal
of the evaluation cannot move the real challenge's high-water mark. (This is the failure that
contaminated PropSim's ledger; it is designed out here.)

## 3. Architecture

```
Engine/            pure C#, netstandard2.0, LangVersion 7.3, zero NinjaTrader references
  PropRules.cs       the rule record + enums
  RuleCatalog.cs     the LucidPro catalog + the three not-modelled firms
  SessionClock.cs    trading-day bucketing (18:00 ET open, 16:45 ET ratchet)
  TradingDay.cs      one day: date, realized P&L, closing balance, fills
  ChallengeState.cs  the computed answer: floor, headroom, verdict, binding constraint
  ChallengeEngine.cs the computation, deterministic and side-effect free
  AccountBinding.cs  NT8 account -> firm/plan/size/phase/options, XML round-trip
NinjaTrader/       the add-on, deployed to bin/Custom/AddOns/FundedPath/
  FundedPathAddOn.cs    Control Center menu registration
  FundedPathWindow.cs   NTWindow + IWorkspacePersistence + tab factory
  FundedPathTab.cs      the NTTabPage: selectors, rail, chart host, account subscription
  CurveChart.cs      custom-drawn growth curve with hover readout
  BindingDialog.cs   map an NT8 account to a challenge
Tests/             xunit, net8.0, references Engine
docs/              this spec, rule sources, images
```

Both `Engine/*.cs` and `NinjaTrader/*.cs` are copied **flat** into
`…/NinjaTrader 8/bin/Custom/AddOns/FundedPath/`. NinjaTrader compiles every `.cs` under
`bin/Custom` into one assembly, so the engine ships as source, exactly like Liquidity Radar.

**C# level: 7.3.** No `switch` expressions, no target-typed `new`, no records, no nullable reference
types, no string interpolation with `$"..."` beyond what 7.3 allows (interpolation itself is fine).

## 4. Engine contract

> *Amended 2026-08-22 (A4, A6). The shipped `PropRules` adds `DailyLossLimitWhenOn`,
> `PeakEodCloseSeed` and the computed helpers `FloorLockLevel` / `InitialFloor` / `SeededHwm` /
> `SeededFloor` / `TargetBalance` / `PayoutBalance` / `TrailStopClose` / `HasDailyLossLimit` /
> `MaxMicros`, and renames `ScaleDllPctOfPeak` to `ScaleDllPctOfPeakProfit`. `ChallengeState` and
> `TradingDay` ship exactly as written below. `ChallengeState.Warnings` is ordered: the engine's own
> computed alarms first, the catalog's rule prose appended last, because the tab renders only the
> first four.*

```csharp
namespace FundedPath.Engine
{
    public enum Firm         { Lucid, MyFundedFutures, ApexTrader, TopstepTrader }
    public enum Phase        { Evaluation, LiveSim, Live }
    public enum HwmBasis     { EodClose, IntradayEquity }
    public enum BreachBasis  { Equity, Balance }
    public enum Verdict      { InProgress, Passed, PayoutEligible, DailyLockout, Breached, Untracked }

    public sealed class PropRules
    {
        public Firm    Firm            { get; set; }
        public string  Plan            { get; set; }   // "LucidPro"
        public Phase   Phase           { get; set; }
        public int     Size            { get; set; }   // 50000
        public double  StartBalance    { get; set; }
        public double  ProfitTarget    { get; set; }   // 0 when the phase has none
        public double  MaxLoss         { get; set; }
        public HwmBasis HwmBasis       { get; set; }
        public double  FloorLockOffset { get; set; }   // floor stops at StartBalance + this
        public double  DailyLossLimit  { get; set; }   // 0 = off / none
        public bool    DailyLossSoft   { get; set; }
        public double  ConsistencyPct  { get; set; }   // 0 = none; 40 = 40%
        public bool    ConsistencyBlocksPayoutOnly { get; set; }
        public double  Buffer          { get; set; }   // 0 = n/a
        public double  MinPayout       { get; set; }
        public double  ScaleDllPctOfPeak { get; set; } // informational only, 0 = n/a
                                                      // (A3: shipped as ScaleDllPctOfPeakProfit)
        public int     MaxContracts    { get; set; }   // minis
        public int     MicroRatio      { get; set; }   // 10
        public int     MinDays         { get; set; }
        public int     DaysToPayout    { get; set; }
        public bool    Verified        { get; set; }
        public string[] Notes          { get; set; }
        public string  SourceUrl       { get; set; }
        public bool    Modelled        { get; set; }   // false for the three other firms
    }

    public sealed class TradingDay
    {
        public DateTime Date           { get; set; }   // the ET trading-day date
        public double   RealizedPnL    { get; set; }
        public double   ClosingBalance { get; set; }
        public int      Fills          { get; set; }
        public double   FloorInForce   { get; set; }   // the floor that applied DURING this day
    }

    public sealed class ChallengeState
    {
        public Verdict  Verdict            { get; set; }
        public string   Headline           { get; set; }   // "ON TRACK · $390 TO PASS"
        public string   BindingConstraint  { get; set; }   // what breaks first, in words
        public double   Balance            { get; set; }   // realized
        public double   Equity             { get; set; }   // realized + unrealized
        public double   Floor              { get; set; }
        public bool     FloorLocked        { get; set; }
        public double   RoomToFloor        { get; set; }   // uses BreachBasis
        public double   ToTarget           { get; set; }
        public double   ProgressPct        { get; set; }
        public double   DayPnL             { get; set; }
        public double   BestDayPnL         { get; set; }
        public double   ConsistencyCapNow  { get; set; }   // 0 = rule absent
        public bool     ConsistencyOk      { get; set; }
        public int      QualifyingDays     { get; set; }
        public IReadOnlyList<TradingDay> Days { get; set; }
        public string[] Warnings           { get; set; }   // unverified-rule flags
    }

    public static class ChallengeEngine
    {
        // days must be ordered oldest-first and carry RealizedPnL; ClosingBalance and FloorInForce
        // are filled in by this call.
        public static ChallengeState Evaluate(
            PropRules rules,
            IReadOnlyList<TradingDay> days,   // completed days only, oldest first
            double openDayRealized,           // realized P&L of the day in progress
            double openUnrealized,            // unrealized P&L of open positions
            BreachBasis breachBasis);
    }
}
```

### 4.1 Floor algorithm (the part that must be exactly right)

```
floor(day i) = min( max(closes[0..i-1]) - MaxLoss , StartBalance + FloorLockOffset )
```
- `closes[-1]` is `SeededHwm = max(StartBalance, PeakEodCloseSeed)`, so day 0's floor is
  `min(SeededHwm - MaxLoss, StartBalance + FloorLockOffset)`. With no seed that is
  `StartBalance - MaxLoss`, which is the form this section was approved with.
  *(Amended 2026-08-22, A5. Without the seed an account bound mid-challenge restarted its
  high-water mark at the start balance and reported a floor BELOW the firm's.)*
- The high-water mark uses **closes strictly before day i** — the floor shown for a day is the floor
  that was in force *during* that day. The ratchet from day i's own close applies from day i+1.
- Once `floor == StartBalance + FloorLockOffset`, it is frozen forever: later closes never move it,
  and a drawdown never lowers it.
- The live floor (for the day in progress) uses all completed closes.

### 4.2 Verdict precedence

1. `Breached` — the breach-basis value is at or below the floor. Terminal; nothing overrides it.
2. `DailyLockout` — DLL is on and the day's loss reached it. Soft: the account survives.
   *(Amended 2026-08-22, A2: "on" means bought ON **and armed** — the fixed limit is disarmed once
   the balance reaches `TrailStopClose`, where the firm hands over to the unenforced LucidScale
   limit. A warning says so on the state.)*
3. `Passed` (Evaluation) — balance ≥ StartBalance + ProfitTarget **and** MinDays met.
4. `PayoutEligible` (LiveSim) — balance ≥ Buffer + MinPayout **and** consistency satisfied
   **and** DaysToPayout met.
5. `InProgress` otherwise.

`BindingConstraint` names whichever of {target, floor, consistency, days} has the least slack,
expressed in dollars where a dollar figure exists.

## 5. NinjaTrader layer contract

- **Menu:** Control Center → New → "Funded Path", registered in `OnWindowCreated`, removed in
  `OnWindowDestroyed`, opened through `Globals.RandomDispatcher.InvokeAsync`. Copy the Liquidity
  Radar pattern verbatim.
- **Account data:** `Account.All` under `lock (Account.All)`; subscribe `AccountItemUpdate` and
  `ExecutionUpdate`; unsubscribe in `Cleanup()`.
- **Day series:** `Cbi.SystemPerformance.Calculate(account.Executions.ToList()).AllTrades` gives
  closed trades with `Entry`/`Exit` executions and `ProfitCurrency`. Bucket each trade by the ET
  trading day of its **exit** time, sum `ProfitCurrency`, and accumulate into closing balances.
- **Live numbers:** `Account.Get(AccountItem.CashValue, Currency.UsDollar)` for balance and
  `Account.Get(AccountItem.UnrealizedProfitLoss, Currency.UsDollar)` for open P&L.
- **Clock:** the replay clock belongs to a **replay account**, not to the platform. Use
  `Connection.PlaybackConnection.Now` only when the account being measured is itself a Playback
  account (`Account.Provider == Provider.Playback`); every other account ages against
  `Core.Globals.Now`, even while a Playback connection is open somewhere in NinjaTrader. A Playback
  rehearsal must still never age against the wall clock — the replay can run at 24x or sit paused
  for an hour.
  *(Amended 2026-08-22. The contract as approved read
  `Connection.PlaybackConnection != null ? Connection.PlaybackConnection.Now : Core.Globals.Now`,
  which is unconditional: with Market Replay merely connected, a real broker account's day would be
  bucketed under the replay's date, mis-dating the day in progress and writing that date into the
  real account's day ledger.)*
- **Timezone:** platform times come in `Core.Globals.GeneralOptions.TimeZoneInfo`. Convert to ET
  before bucketing. Cache the ET `TimeZoneInfo`; fall back to a fixed −5/−4 offset if the id is
  missing on the machine.
- **Threading:** account events arrive on a background thread. Never touch WPF from them — set a
  volatile snapshot and repaint from a `DispatcherTimer` at 4 Hz. This window has no hot path.
- **Persistence — two files**, both under `%USERPROFILE%/Documents/NinjaTrader 8/FundedPath/`,
  both written with `System.Xml.Linq` and both written temp-then-swap (each therefore also produces
  a transient `.tmp` and leaves the previous version as `.bak`). No third-party JSON dependency.
  - `bindings.xml` — every account binding, one file for all accounts.
  - `days-<ledger key>.xml` — **one per bound account**, the ledger of completed trading days
    (`date`, `pnl`, `fills`). *(Amended 2026-08-22: the contract as approved named one file.)*
    This one is not a cache and cannot be dropped. `Account.Executions` is the current session only
    — `Account.LookbackDaysExecutions == 3` — so a 20-day evaluation cannot be rebuilt from the
    platform after an NT8 restart, and a missing green day lowers the high-water mark, lowers the
    floor and reports **more** room than the trader has. That is the dangerous direction, so
    completed days are persisted and only the day in progress is derived from executions. Computed
    days win over stored ones for the days they cover, so a broker amendment or a Playback rewind
    corrects the ledger instead of accumulating into it.
  - The ledger key is the binding key (`Provider + "|" + AccountDisplayName`) with the separator and
    any character illegal in a filename replaced by `_`, which is what keeps a Playback rehearsal's
    ledger out of the real account's file.
  - Where the executions cannot reach far enough back — an account bound mid-challenge — the binding
    carries `PeakEodCloseSeed`, the trader's own highest end-of-day close, typed in the binding
    dialog. It seeds the high-water mark as a lower bound (`SeededHwm = max(StartBalance,
    PeakEodCloseSeed)`), and the engine starts its ratchet there. Without it the floor restarts from
    the start balance and reads low, which is the optimistic error.
    *(Amended 2026-08-22, A5: the contract as approved had no seed at all.)*
  - The binding also carries `StartedUtc`, the **first day counted**: ledger days that closed before
    it are dropped from the challenge, and `DateTime.MinValue` means "count every day in the ledger".
    *(Amended 2026-08-22, A9: §7 q4 offered reading it from the first fill instead.)*
- **Never place, modify or cancel an order.** This add-on is read-only on the account, by design and
  in the README. It has no order-entry surface at all.
